\chapter{Control flows}%

\label{chap:flow}

Control flow constructs decide what the body of a function does, and in what
order. They range from a simple conditional branch to iteration over a collection
and deconstruction by pattern matching. This chapter specifies each of them.

Every construct in this chapter is local: it appears inside a function body and
nowhere else.
