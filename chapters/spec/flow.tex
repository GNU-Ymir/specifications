\chapter{Control flows}%

\label{chap:flow}

Control flow constructs decide what the body of a function does, and in what
order. This chapter specifies blocks, conditional branching, the three loops
\token{loop}, \token{while} and \token{for}, the jumps \token{break} and
\token{continue}, and pattern matching. 

Every construct in this chapter is local: it appears inside a function body and
nowhere else. Every construct is also an expression, with a type and possibly a
value, so each section follows the same plan: \textit{Grammar}, then the
\textit{Evaluation} rules, then the \textit{Value and type} of the construct,
then \textit{Diagnostics}, the errors the compiler reports when a rule is
broken. A section adds subsections only for what the construct has of its own.
