\chapter{Notation}%
\label{chap:notation}

Part II specifies, in precise terms, the language that Part I teaches. Where
Part I shows what a construction is for, Part II states every rule it obeys,
and the diagnostics the compiler reports when a rule is broken. Its chapters
follow the language rather than the order of the course. This chapter defines the conventions that all the chapters of this part
share: the notation of the grammar productions, and the form of the listings.
