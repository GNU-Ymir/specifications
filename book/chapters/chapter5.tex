\chapter{Global constructions}%
\label{chap:global}

A source file is a list of global declarations, written one after another with
nothing enclosing them. This chapter specifies what may appear at that outermost
level: the module structure, the semantics of importation, and the other global
symbols.

Three kinds of global declaration are specified elsewhere, each having enough
behaviour of its own to need a chapter: custom types in
Chapter~\ref{chap:custom_types}, macros in Chapter~\ref{chap:macros}, and pragmas
and the rest of conditional compilation in
Chapter~\ref{chap:conditional_compilation}.
